# Ex.No.-1.1-Design and Draft the given 2D Sketches in modelling software.

## AIM

To sketch the given drawing with dimensions using fusion 360 tool.

## REQUIREMENTS

  1. Autodesk fusion 360
  2. Windows 10
  3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 2 GB or more)
  4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 
  
## PROCEDURE

1. Open Fusion 360, create new design

5. using same tool  Draw inner circle of Ø40 at same origin.
 
6. Draw one circle of R20 with its centre on Ø66 circle on horizontal centre line.
  
7. Select R20 circle and apply Circular Pattern with centre as origin, quantity 6

9. use trim option

11.continue the steps

12. Draw top circle of Ø32 with centre at 86 mm above origin on vertical centre line. Draw hole circle of Ø20 at same centre.

13. Draw right side circle of Ø20 with centre at 47 mm right and 26 mm below origin. Draw hole circle of Ø14 at same centre.

14. Draw bottom circle of Ø20 with centre at 50 mm below origin on vertical centre line. Draw hole circle of Ø10 at same centre.
15 Draw tangent arc of R64 between top Ø32 circle and centre Ø66 circle on right side.

17. Draw tangent arc of R20 between centre Ø66 circle and right side Ø20 circle.
 
18. Draw tangent arc of R20 between right side Ø20 circle and bottom Ø20 circle.
 
19. Select all right side geometry and apply Mirror about vertical centre line to create left side geometry.
